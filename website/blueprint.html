Notation and conventions
Time
a <= t <= b inside the supplied Ricci-flow time set; high-order evolution is proved at interior times.
Space
x is a period-one real parameter; D_s = v^{-1}D_x.
Metric
All tensor norms, pairings and curve quantities use the actual g(t) or its genuine product metric.
Curvature sign
rm uses the upstream intrinsic four-form; its conversion from the opposite-sign chart curvature operator is explicitly proved.
Constants
B bounds Ricci, K bounds Riemann curvature, D bounds covariant Ricci; A=max(6B+2K,6D), C=A/2+B.
Regularization
epsilon > 0; Theta_epsilon is compared first and epsilon tends to zero afterwards.

  • Inputs are the one-dimensional manifold, its starting metric, existing smooth curve flows and uniform initial bounds.
  • The construction uses positive scales. A concrete AddCircle atlas, ramps and solution existence belong to the surrounding application; no metric at scale zero is constructed.
