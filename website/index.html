Uniform estimates over static one-dimensional factors

For a compact base Ricci flow on [a,b], choose constants before all static one-dimensional factor metrics, curve families and initial bounds. Curves are existing smooth immersed periodic solutions. The positive-scale corollary permits arbitrary metrics indexed by lambda > 0.

Scope of the result

The formalization assumes an existing smooth, immersed, closed curve flow and proves the internal geometric evolution identities and analytic estimates controlling length and total curvature.

The geometric quantities use the actual evolving metric. Uniform product bounds use constants obtained from the compact base geometry, independent of the static one-dimensional factor metric. Regularization is removed after comparison.
