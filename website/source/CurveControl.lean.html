47# Uniform curve control under Ricci flow
48
49The root imports the full geometric and analytic proof chain, compact-ambient
50tensor bounds, and the uniform static one-dimensional factor application.
51Smooth immersed curve-shortening flow existence and the initial data remain
52inputs. Internal evolution, regularization, integration, comparison, and the
53auxiliary-factor independence of constants are proved in these modules.
54
55See Geometry.UniformProductEstimates for the compact-base final theorem and
56the positive-scale-indexed corollary. Audit.lean checks all imported project
57declarations and their reachable axioms; semantic reviews are in docs/.
